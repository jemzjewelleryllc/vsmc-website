🔒 Privacy & Consent

Your Privacy Rights Under PHIPA

The Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA) protects your health information. At VSMC, we are committed to safeguarding your privacy.

  • Why we collect information: To provide you with appropriate medical care and treatment.
  • Who has access: Only healthcare providers directly involved in your care (your "circle of care").
  • Your rights: You may access, correct, or withdraw consent for your health information at any time.
  • Data protection: All information is stored securely with encryption and access controls.
