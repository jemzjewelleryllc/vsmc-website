Privacy Policy

Your privacy is protected by Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA)

1. Introduction

VSMC - Very Specific Medical Centre ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal health information in accordance with the Personal Health Information Protection Act, 2004 (PHIPA) of Ontario.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of personal health information:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, date of birth, address, phone number, email address
  • Health Card Information: Ontario Health Card number (optional)
  • Medical History: Current conditions, medications, allergies, previous treatments
  • Treatment Information: Diagnoses, treatment plans, appointment records
  • Billing Information: Insurance details, payment records

3. How We Use Your Information

Your personal health information is used for:

  • Providing healthcare services and treatment
  • Communicating with you about appointments and care
  • Sharing information within your circle of care
  • Processing billing and insurance claims
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements

4. Circle of Care

Under PHIPA, your health information may be shared with healthcare providers directly involved in your care ("circle of care") without additional consent. This includes:

  • Your treating physicians at VSMC
  • Specialists to whom you are referred
  • Pharmacies dispensing your prescriptions
  • Laboratories performing diagnostic tests

You may withdraw consent for sharing within the circle of care at any time, though this may affect the quality of care we can provide.

5. Your Rights Under PHIPA

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your health records
  • Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate information
  • Withdraw Consent: Limit or withdraw consent for the use of your information
  • Complaint: File a complaint with the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario

6. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure electronic medical record systems
  • Role-based access controls
  • Regular security audits and staff training
  • Physical security of paper records

7. Data Retention

We retain your health records for a minimum of 10 years from the date of last entry (or until age 28 for minors), as required by Ontario regulations. After this period, records are securely destroyed.
